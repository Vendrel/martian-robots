# NASA rover imagery: permitted and prohibited uses

Last verified: 2026-09-15  
Scope: Curiosity, Perseverance, Spirit, and Opportunity images and associated
NASA/PDS source material used by this project.  This is an implementation
checklist, not legal advice.

## Permitted

- Use NASA-provided rover images and media factually or educationally in the
  app, documentation, simulations, exhibits, and web pages; NASA says such
  content is generally not subject to U.S. copyright and generally permits
  factual use that does not imply endorsement.
- Include a truthful source acknowledgement, e.g. `Image: NASA/JPL-Caltech`,
  and preserve a more specific credit supplied with the image record (such as
  additional instrument partners).
- Use imagery editorially in a non-promotional published work.  A NASA
  identifier that is already visible in a factual photograph may normally stay
  visible in that factual context.
- Link to NASA pages and use a compliant NASA image as a graphic link to a NASA
  page, while still giving NASA credit and avoiding an endorsement implication.

## Prohibited or requires separate clearance

- Do not use an item marked as copyrighted by a third party without permission
  from that named rightsholder.  NASA's own use of third-party material grants
  no downstream rights.  Check each gallery record, image page, PDS label, and
  embedded credit before publication.
- Do not state or imply that NASA or a NASA employee endorses, sponsors,
  approves, co-created, or is otherwise affiliated with this app or a product.
  In particular, do not call it “official NASA”, “NASA approved”, or similar.
- Do not use the NASA Insignia (meatball), Logotype (worm), Seal, or other NASA
  identifiers as this project's branding, advertising, trade dress, or
  promotional graphic without NASA's explicit permission.  Do not extract an
  identifier from a rover photo for separate use.
- For advertising, promotional content, merchandise, or product packaging,
  obtain the relevant NASA clearance first when imagery includes NASA logos or
  personnel; commercial use must never imply endorsement.  Current NASA
  employee/astronaut names or likenesses have additional restrictions.
- Do not use NASA imagery in connection with NFTs or claim copyright, exclusive
  ownership, or other rights in NASA material that the project does not have.
- If images are used to train or appear in an AI product, do not attribute the
  AI output to NASA or place NASA identifiers with AI-generated imagery; disclose
  the NASA source factually without implying NASA permission or review.

## Project rule

Store the exact source URL/product ID and source credit with every downloaded
image.  Render that credit in the image details/export, block assets with a
third-party copyright notice until cleared, and keep project branding separate
from NASA identifiers.
